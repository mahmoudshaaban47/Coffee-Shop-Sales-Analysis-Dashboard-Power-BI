# Coffee-Shop-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard-Power-BI
Turning coffee shop sales data into business insights ✨


The goal of this project was to analyze sales trends and product performance to support better sales and profitability decisions.

The analysis revealed consistent daily sales with clear peaks on Mondays and Fridays, alongside a strong upward trend from January to June, with June as the peak month. 

A small group of products generated most of the revenue, while several items underperformed.


📊 Business Impact:

 • Clear seasonal sales patterns and peak periods
 
 • Best-selling products are the main revenue drivers
 
 • Underperforming products need optimization or repositioning
 
 • Limited customer data restricts deeper behavior analysis


☑️ Business recommendations:

 • Run targeted promotions and increase staffing during peak periods
 
 • Ensure sufficient stock for top-selling products
 
 • Improve visibility and bundling for underperforming items
 
 • Collect customer data to enable personalized marketing
